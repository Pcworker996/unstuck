# 04 — Save a protocol and report its Agency shift

**What to build:** A person can choose whether a Check-in becomes lasting history, select and perform a Pivot themselves, and report a Pivot outcome plus an optional Agency shift. Saved history is inspectable and deletable; an unsaved or incomplete protocol leaves no lasting personal memory.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Collaborate through a versioned, editable Situation map.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Every Check-in exposes a clear save choice, and the person can complete the protocol without saving it.
- [ ] The person can select, regenerate, or dismiss a Pivot; Unstuck records the choice but does not perform the Pivot on the person's behalf.
- [ ] The person can record exactly one outcome of completed, partly helpful, not a fit, or skipped.
- [ ] Agency shift is optional and limited to more-able, about-as-able, or less-able; no numeric emotional-state or wellness score remains in the active experience.
- [ ] A saved Check-in can retain its Private entry, versioned Situation map, selected Pivot, outcome, optional Agency shift, and a compact Derived memory approved by the person.
- [ ] The person can inspect and delete saved Check-ins and Derived memories, with owner isolation enforced for every operation.
- [ ] Unsaved, interrupted, incomplete, or invalid protocols create no lasting user history or Derived memory.
- [ ] Outcome submission and memory enrichment are idempotent; enrichment failure preserves the accepted outcome and reports that adaptation is unavailable.
- [ ] Tests cover saved and unsaved paths, every outcome and Agency-shift value, deletion, isolation, duplicate submission, and incomplete-run exclusion.
- [ ] The interface explains what will be saved before consent and makes retained state inspectable afterward.
